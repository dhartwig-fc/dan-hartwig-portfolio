Over Invoicing

Are import or export transactions being deliberately over-invoiced to facilitate the movement of illicit value across borders?

Over-invoicing is a common Trade-Based Money Laundering technique whereby the price of goods is intentionally inflated beyond their true market value.

The excess value embedded within the invoice enables the covert transfer of funds between counterparties while appearing to represent legitimate trade activity.

Common objectives include:

  • Laundering criminal proceeds
  • Capital flight
  • Tax evasion
  • Sanctions circumvention
  • Hidden value transfer between related parties
