NI006 · Network Intelligence

Common Contact

Do two or more entities share the same contact details?

Supporting questions:

Do entities share phone numbers?

Do entities share email addresses?

Do entities share domains?

Do entities share operational contacts?

Does the contact indicate common control?

Does the contact create additional risk?

NI006 Common Contact identifies entities connected through shared contact information.

The purpose of this intelligence product is to detect relationships that may indicate:

  • Common control
  • Related-party activity
  • Undisclosed relationships
  • Coordinated activity
  • Shell company networks
  • Ownership concealment
  • Financial crime exposure

Common contacts often provide strong evidence of operational connectivity between entities that appear independent.

Risk indicators

Risk

  • Shared email across multiple entities
  • Shared phone number across counterparties
  • Contact linked to sanctioned entity
  • Operational connectivity indicators
  • High-risk contact cluster detected
