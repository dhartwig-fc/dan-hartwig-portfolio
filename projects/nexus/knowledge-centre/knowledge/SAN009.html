SAN009 · Sanctions

Complex Ownership Structures

Complex Ownership Structures use structural opacity itself as the evasion mechanism: deep layering, circular holdings, nominee tiers and clusters of secrecy-jurisdiction incorporations engineered so that no reader can trace ultimate control. The structure need not resolve to a named designated party to be a finding — the opacity is the risk.

The finding is a structural finding: how deep the layering runs, whether the ownership graph is circular, how many nominee tiers and secrecy jurisdictions appear, and how concentrated the network is around shared directors and addresses.

Risk indicators

Structural

  • Layering depth ≥ 4–5
  • Circular ownership loop
  • Two or more nominee tiers

Network-Concentration

  • Shared directors across nodes
  • Shared registered addresses
  • Multiple shell indicators

Jurisdiction

  • Multiple secrecy jurisdictions (BVI, Cayman, Panama, Seychelles)

Counterparty

  • Counterparties carrying sanctions exposure or adverse media

Investigation actions

  1. Resolve every node and edge of the ownership structure.
  2. Compute layering depth (longest path) and detect circularity (DFS cycle detection).
  3. Count nominee tiers and secrecy jurisdictions.
  4. Grade Ownership Structure from depth / circularity / nominee tiers.
  5. Grade Network Concentration from shared directors, shared addresses, shell indicators.
  6. Score jurisdiction and counterparty exposure.
  7. Aggregate; escalate at or above the threshold.

Detection logic

  1. Resolve every node and edge of the ownership structure.
  2. Compute layering depth (longest path) and detect circularity (DFS cycle detection).
  3. Count nominee tiers and secrecy jurisdictions.
  4. Grade Ownership Structure from depth / circularity / nominee tiers.
  5. Grade Network Concentration from shared directors, shared addresses, shell indicators.
  6. Score jurisdiction and counterparty exposure.
  7. Aggregate; escalate at or above the threshold.

  1. Ownership structure

    found

    What the engine statesThe engine's output gives the number of entities in the structure as 6. It states that the structure contains a circular holding, and gives its depth as 6, which is the number of entities in it. It counts 2 of the structure's ownership links as nominee links

    The ruleOwnership structure: the structure is every entity joined to the subject by ownership links, whichever way they point. A circular holding, or a longest ownership chain of five or more links, is Critical; otherwise a chain of four or more links, or three or more nominee links, is High; otherwise a chain of three or more links, or two or more nominee links, is Medium; otherwise Low. A nominee link is an ownership link whose control type is nominee. The engine records the longest chain as the structure's depth; for a circular structure it records the number of entities instead. Engine band: Critical. This component carries 45% of the score: 40.5 points

An address shared by more than one of them is one signal, however many there are. Two or more entities flagged as shell companies are two signals, and exactly one is one signal. Four or more signals is Critical; otherwise three is High; otherwise one or two is Medium; otherwise Low. Engine band: Critical. This component carries 25% of the score: 22.5 points
