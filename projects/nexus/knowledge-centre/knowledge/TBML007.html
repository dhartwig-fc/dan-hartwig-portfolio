Dual Use Goods

Is a trade moving a dual-use or export-controlled commodity — and does its end-use, technology content and destination indicate diversion to a prohibited purpose? ---

Dual Use Goods are items with a legitimate civilian use that can also serve a military or proliferation purpose — electronics, precision instruments, controlled technology. When such goods move through trade, the risk turns on the commodity's classification, its end-use and its destination, not merely on the parties named. Trade in dual-use goods can be used to divert controlled items to prohibited end-uses under cover of ordinary commerce. It is used to: - Divert controlled goods to military or proliferation end-uses - Move export-controlled technology through intermediaries - Evade export licensing and end-use controls - Support sanctioned or embargoed programmes - Conceal the true end-user behind a civilian pretext ---

Risk narrative

A customer trades a commodity that is export-controlled — dual-use, with a military end-use or controlled-technology content. The trade documents describe a civilian transaction, but the commodity's classification and the destination indicate a controlled item bound for a sensitive end-user. Where the counterparty is a shell and the parties share ownership, the trade is a diversion channel rather than legitimate commerce. ---

Risk indicators

Risk

  • Dual-Use Commodity
  • Military End-Use
  • Controlled Technology
  • High Value Trade
  • Related Party Trading
  • Shell Company Structure

Investigation actions

  1. Confirm the commodity classification, end-use and destination.
  2. Screen the end-user against sanctions and entity lists.
  3. Verify export licensing for the commodity and destination.
  4. Resolve ownership; test for shared directors / owners and related-party trade.
  5. Package evidence for investigator review where the score is High or above.

Use cases

  • Trade Based Money Laundering (TBML)
  • Export-Control / Dual-Use Diversion
  • Proliferation Financing
  • Sanctions Evasion
  • Related Party Value Transfer
