NI007 · Network Intelligence

NI007

```text Does the network contain characteristics consistent with a shell company structure? ``` Supporting questions: ```text Do entities share addresses? Do entities share directors? Do entities share beneficial owners? Do entities share contacts? Do entities have limited operational footprint? Do multiple risk indicators converge? ``` ---

NI007 Shell Company Network identifies networks of entities exhibiting characteristics commonly associated with shell companies. The purpose of this intelligence product is to detect networks that may be used to: - Conceal beneficial ownership - Facilitate money laundering - Enable trade-based financial crime - Evade sanctions - Obscure ownership structures - Hide related-party activity - Support fraud schemes NI007 combines multiple Network Intelligence products into a higher-order intelligence finding. ---
