Platform Roadmap

From one substrate to an operating platform.

A bank runs sanctions, transaction monitoring and case management as three separate systems, with an intelligence function above and investigators below. NEXUS connects them at the substrate — one resolved graph carrying identity, ownership, risk, typology and money flow, so every layer is a view on one truth rather than a system to reconcile. The detection chain and the fused investigator console are built; the arc below is the destination and where it stands today.

The journey

Built

Intelligence foundation

A resolved-entity and typology substrate: the canonical data model, one shared entity key, and the resolved global graph. Every layer above is a view on this.

Built

Detection & scoring

Typologies across the crime families — network, trade-based money laundering, sanctions, correspondent banking and capital markets — with governed risk scoring into a ranked alert table.

Built

Fused investigation

One console where investigation and case management meet on the substrate: the graph, the case lifecycle, and an explainable, provenance-linked SAR draft — investigate and manage on one screen.

Next

Operational surface

A replicable investigator workspace — one alert, one three-panel object (the pattern · the tunable score · the governed decision) — identical for every typology, gated by a full functional test matrix.

Planned

Operations management

The supervisor's layer above execution: management information and KPIs, a SAR lifecycle manager, workflow and SLA orchestration, and quality assurance.

Future

Enterprise & threat intelligence

A threat-intelligence layer feeding the substrate, calibration from real run history, and scale across domains — the reusable financial-crime operating platform.

Guiding principles

One substrate Identity, ownership, risk, typology, sanctions and money flow on one resolved graph — every layer a view on one truth, not a system to reconcile.
Governed & deterministic Every build is versioned, validated and byte-identical to rebuild. Nothing ships without passing the gate.
Evidence led Outputs are structured around evidence, risk, confidence, rationale and the action to take.
Tested to ship Every typology passes a five-layer functional matrix — positive and negative, on full data — before it goes live.
Tunable, not hard-coded Scoring lives in governed, versioned configuration — thresholds and weights are tuning elements, calibrated on evidence.
Private IP protected Public pages show capability and architecture while the proprietary analytics and implementation stay private.

The chain is built — the work now is the operational surface.

The near-term focus is the replicable investigator workspace and rolling it across every typology, then the operations-management layer above it. The destination is a reusable financial-crime intelligence operating platform that connects analytics, entity resolution, investigation and governance on a single substrate.
