Release Notes · Beta v2.2

What's new in Beta v2.2.

The core build is the investigator console: one resolved-entity substrate fusing transaction monitoring, sanctions and case management, with governed typologies, doctrine and scoring. Beta v2.2 is what has been built on top of it since then, all on synthetic data. The full delivery record, wave by wave, is in the stage catalogue.

Updated 25 November 2026 · Beta v2.2 · 14,990 tests passed, 0 failed, CI-gated

Worked walks on 41 more typology pagesIn the Typology Library, 41 more typology pages now carry a worked walk, “How this pattern looks”: the sanctions typologies SAN001 to SAN009, the correspondent-banking typologies BA001 to BA010, the trade-based laundering typologies TBML001 to TBML011 and TBML002U, and the capital-markets typologies CM001 to CM010. Start with TBML001 or CM002. Each step shows the evidence, the rule applied and whether the engine found it, judged by the engine’s own band for that step on a synthetic sample subject. With SAN010 and CMI010, 43 of the 58 typologies now have one. The capital-markets sample now uses names that are obviously fictional. The advisory corroboration recorded on 24 November now shows on the CM002, CMI010, PAT002 and PAT007 pages. No score, band or rank moved. All data is synthetic. Landed on 24 and 25 November, published on 25 November.
