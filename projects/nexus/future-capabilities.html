Future Capabilities

Where the AI plugs in.

The chain is built. The next layer makes it learn and stay current — a doctrine foundation plus two AI tracks, all on the same resolved-entity substrate: the Knowledge Centre (the typology doctrine, surfaced), an AI copilot that plugs applications onto grounded context, and a threat-intelligence layer that keeps the platform current. All grounded and governed — AI acting on the platform's own evidence, never a free-floating model.

Direction & architecture · Knowledge Centre is the near-term first step · SAR drafter is copilot slice 1 (live)
Foundation · Knowledge Centre

The doctrine, surfaced — near-term, from what's already written.

The estate already holds written doctrine for all 50 typologies across the analytics toolkits — every method's description, red-flag indicators, worked example and investigation actions. The Knowledge Centre curates them into a governed typology library and surfaces it in the console: an in-context slide-over from any typology chip on a case (the investigator learns why this fired without leaving it), and a standalone Knowledge tab to browse the whole doctrine by family.

Why it comes first — and why it's the spine

It's the static half of the intelligence layer ("what each method is") to threat intelligence's dynamic half ("what's happening now"). Cheap and near-term — the content is already written — and each library record carries an intel attachment point, so Track 2's advisories, actors and emergent typologies pin straight onto it without rework. Build the doctrine, and the live intelligence has something to attach to.

Track 1 · AI Copilot

Plugging AI applications onto grounded context.

The copilot isn't one model — it's a plug. For each task, an engine assembles a governed context package from the substrate (the case, the graph, the scores, the typology doctrine); an AI application acts on that package; the output comes back provenance-cited. The apps are swappable; the grounding stays constant. The SAR drafter proves the pattern end to end.

The principle — swappable apps, constant grounding

The context service is the plug; the AI applications are interchangeable behind it. That's what keeps the copilot governed and auditable: every answer traces to the same substrate, so a better model is an upgrade, not a rebuild — and nothing the copilot says is ungrounded.

Track 2 · Threat Intelligence

Keeping the platform current — curated core, live feeds.

Detection knows the methods; threat intelligence knows what's happening now. This layer pins live intelligence to the typology library and feeds new indicators back into detection — a closed loop. Its architecture is the point: a curated, governed core that's deterministic today, with live-ready ingestion seams for the feeds (the "scraping") that run as runtime operations, outside the hermetic core.

The architecture — "curated core, live-ready seams"

The governed content is committed and byte-identical to rebuild, now. The feed adapters — bulletins, watchlists, adverse media — are runtime ops, lazy-imported, exactly like the live Neo4j service: the platform demonstrates the whole capability deterministically, and the real feeds bolt on at the seam without touching the core.
