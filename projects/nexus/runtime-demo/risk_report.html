Defect: STAGE287-D183. Two different ageing measures exist for the same cases, days since opening and time in the current state, so ageing figures from different views will not agree.
Defect: STAGE287-D216. On the living estate a case for an off-boarded entity stays open and keeps breaching its deadline after its alert is dropped, so breaches can be overstated.
