NI009 · Network Intelligence

Related Party Trading

Are trading counterparties connected through ownership, control or network relationships?

Supporting questions:

Do trading entities share ownership?

Do trading entities share directors?

Do trading entities share addresses?

Do trading entities share contacts?

Does the relationship appear disclosed?

Does the relationship increase financial crime risk?

NI009 Related Party Trading identifies trading activity occurring between entities that are connected through ownership, control, directors, addresses, contacts or other network relationships.

The purpose of this intelligence product is to detect:

  • Undisclosed related-party trading
  • Artificial trade activity
  • Trade-based financial crime
  • Circular trade arrangements
  • Transfer pricing risk
  • Sanctions exposure
  • Trade network manipulation

NI009 represents one of the most important bridges between FC-03 Network Intelligence and FC-04 TBML Analytics.

Risk indicators

Risk

  • Shared beneficial ownership
  • Undisclosed trade relationship
  • Circular trade flow
  • Cross-border ownership structure
  • Indirect sanctions exposure
