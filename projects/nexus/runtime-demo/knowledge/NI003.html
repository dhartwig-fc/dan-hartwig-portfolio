NI003 · Network Intelligence

Layered Ownership

Does ownership pass through multiple intermediate entities before reaching the ultimate beneficial owner?

Supporting questions:

How many ownership layers exist?

Who is the ultimate beneficial owner?

Does the structure reduce transparency?

Does the ownership path cross jurisdictions?

Does the ownership chain create sanctions exposure?

Does the structure increase risk?

NI003 Layered Ownership identifies ownership structures where one or more intermediate entities exist between the ultimate beneficial owner and the subject entity.

The purpose of this intelligence product is to detect complex ownership structures that may be used to:

  • Conceal ultimate ownership
  • Obscure control relationships
  • Reduce transparency
  • Circumvent sanctions controls
  • Hide related-party relationships
  • Increase financial crime risk

Layered ownership is one of the strongest indicators of ownership complexity within a network.

Risk indicators

Risk

  • Three-layer ownership structure
  • Cross-border ownership path
  • Offshore holding company
  • Reduced ownership transparency
  • Potential ownership concealment

Investigation actions

  1. Ownership chains greater than one layer
  2. Multi-jurisdiction ownership structures
  3. Complex holding company arrangements
  4. Nested ownership structures
  5. Cross-border ownership chains
  6. Ownership networks designed to obscure control

Detection logic

NI003 should identify:

Ownership chains greater than one layer

Multi-jurisdiction ownership structures

Complex holding company arrangements

Nested ownership structures

Cross-border ownership chains

Ownership networks designed to obscure control
