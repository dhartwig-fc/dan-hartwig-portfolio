Circular Ownership

Does ownership eventually loop back to the originating entity?

Supporting questions:

Who ultimately controls the structure?

How many entities participate in the ownership cycle?

Is the circular ownership intentional?

Does the structure obscure beneficial ownership?

Does the structure create sanctions exposure?

Does the structure indicate elevated financial crime risk?

NI004 Circular Ownership identifies ownership structures where ownership relationships ultimately loop back to an originating entity.

The purpose of this intelligence product is to detect ownership cycles that may be used to:

  • Conceal beneficial ownership
  • Obscure control structures
  • Inflate balance sheet positions
  • Create artificial ownership independence
  • Hide related-party relationships
  • Reduce ownership transparency

Circular ownership structures are generally considered higher-risk because they can make control relationships difficult to identify.

  • Beneficial ownership obscured
  • Ownership transparency reduced
  • Cross-jurisdiction ownership cycle
