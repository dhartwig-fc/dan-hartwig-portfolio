Under Invoicing

Is a trade transaction being used to transfer value through invoice **deflation** below expected market value — understating the price to move value to the importer, evade duty, or settle an obligation outside the visible trade?

Under Invoicing occurs when the value of goods or services on an invoice is intentionally **understated below** their true market value. It is the mirror of over-invoicing: where over-invoicing moves value to the *exporter* through overpayment, under-invoicing moves value to the *importer* — the buyer receives goods worth far more than the price paid, and the difference is settled or realised outside the visible trade.

It is used to:

  • Move illicit value across borders under the appearance of legitimate trade
  • Evade customs duties and import taxes
  • Facilitate capital flight into an importing jurisdiction
  • Settle obligations off-book between related parties
  • Conceal beneficial ownership and control

Risk narrative

A customer imports goods from a counterparty at a price materially below the market benchmark. The trade documents are internally consistent, but the declared value cannot be reconciled with the commodity's benchmark price. The value gap accrues to the importer; it may be settled through a separate channel, netted against an unrelated obligation, or simply realised on resale at market price. Where the counterparty is a shell and the parties share ownership, the under-price is not a commercial discount — it is a transfer mechanism.

Risk indicators

Risk

  • Invoice Deflation
  • High Value Trade
  • High Risk Corridor
  • Related Party Trading
  • Shell Company Structure
  • Repeat Activity

Investigation actions

  1. Reconcile the declared unit price against the benchmark; confirm the commodity,
  2. Quantify the value gap and establish where it accrues.
  3. Assess counterparty commercial substance; test for shell indicators.
  4. Resolve ownership; test for shared directors / owners and related-party trade.
  5. Trace the payment trail for netting, split or third-party settlement.
  6. Package evidence for investigator review where the score is High or above.

Use cases

  • Trade Based Money Laundering (TBML)
  • Capital Flight
  • Customs / Duty Evasion
  • Related Party Trading
  • Shell Company Abuse
  • Sanctions Evasion
  • Tax Evasion
