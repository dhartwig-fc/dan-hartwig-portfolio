Shadow Fleet

Does the vessel network exhibit characteristics consistent with shadow fleet activity?

Supporting questions:

Who owns the vessel?

Who controls the vessel?

Has ownership recently changed?

Is ownership obscured?

Does the vessel connect to sanctioned entities?

Does the vessel participate in high-risk maritime behaviour?

NI008 Shadow Fleet identifies networks of vessels, operators, owners, managers and associated entities exhibiting characteristics commonly associated with shadow fleet activity.

The purpose of this intelligence product is to detect maritime networks that may be used to:

  • Evade sanctions
  • Conceal cargo origins
  • Conceal cargo destinations
  • Obscure vessel ownership
  • Facilitate illicit trade
  • Circumvent regulatory controls
  • Enable sanctions evasion schemes

NI008 is a specialised Network Intelligence capability supporting sanctions, trade finance and maritime investigations.

Risk indicators

Shadow Fleet

  • Frequent Flag Changes
  • Frequent Ownership Changes
  • AIS Gaps
  • Ship-to-Ship Transfers
  • Offshore Ownership
  • Layered Ownership
  • Sanctioned Connections
  • High-Risk Trade Routes
  • Opaque Ownership

Risk

  • Layered vessel ownership
  • Frequent flag changes
  • AIS anomalies
  • Indirect sanctions exposure
  • Ownership opacity
