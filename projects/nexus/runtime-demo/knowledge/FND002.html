FND002 · Network Intelligence

Relationship Discovery

What connects these two entities, on what evidence, and how firmly?

The second half matters as much as the first. An edge asserted from a shared telephone number and an edge asserted from a filed directorship are both relationships, and an investigator must not weigh them alike.

FND002 Relationship Discovery is the foundation capability that derives typed, confidence-scored edges between resolved entities from six independent evidence sources, and reduces them to one non-duplicated set.

FND001 establishes who the nodes are. FND002 establishes what connects them. A typology such as PAT001 Shared Director is a question asked of this output — it does not discover the directorship, it interprets one FND002 has already found.

Risk indicators

Risk

  • Shared attribute edge read as evidence of control
  • Dense shared attribute cluster, the signature of a formation agent
  • Control edge sitting at exactly its default confidence
  • Ownership edge sitting at exactly its default confidence
  • Control asserted with no transactional edge between the parties
  • Transaction edge recorded from one side only
  • Edge whose only evidence source is an address match

Investigation actions

  1. Read the relationship category before the confidence score
  2. Treat Control, Ownership, Shared Attribute and Transactional as four different claims
  3. Count how many other entities share the value behind a shared attribute edge
  4. Treat an address shared by ninety entities as a service provider, not a network
  5. Check whether a control edge is supported by any transactional edge
  6. Treat a confidence sitting at exactly its default as absent rather than measured
  7. Read the evidence source recorded on each edge before relying on it

Detection logic

Six discovery passes run over the entity index, each emitting a typed edge:

Director pass emits DIRECTOR_OF, category Control, confidence default 0.95

Ownership pass emits BENEFICIAL_OWNER_OF, category Ownership, confidence default 0.93

Address pass emits SHARES_ADDRESS_WITH, category Shared Attribute, confidence fixed 0.90

Contact pass emits SHARES_CONTACT_WITH, category Shared Attribute, confidence fixed 0.88

Transaction pass emits TRANSACTS_WITH, category Transactional, confidence default 0.90

Trade pass emits TRADES_WITH, category Transactional, confidence default 0.92

The category is the load-bearing distinction, not the score. There are four of them and they answer different questions. Control asserts authority over an entity. Ownership asserts an economic stake, which is what FND003 walks into chains. Shared Attribute asserts only co-occurrence of a value — two companies at one serviced address share an address and may share nothing else. Transactional asserts a dealing. Reading a 0.90 address edge as comparable to a 0.93 ownership edge because the numbers are close is the misreading this list exists to prevent.

Two of the six relationship types are taken from the source record, with the value above as the fallback. An ownership record naming itself SHAREHOLDER_OF or ULTIMATE_OWNER_OF keeps that type, and only an unlabelled one becomes BENEFICIAL_OWNER_OF. A transaction record may likewise name its own type. The other four are fixed by the pass that emits them.

Confidence is carried from the source record for the four record-backed passes, and the defaults above apply only where the record supplies none. A score sitting at exactly its default therefore means the upstream source graded nothing — it is a fallback, not a measurement.

Shared attribute confidence is fixed outright and cannot be overridden, because the evidence is the match itself and admits no per-record grading. An address match is always 0.90 and a contact match always 0.88, never more and never less.

Edges are then deduplicated on the source entity, the target entity, the relationship type, the shared value, the transaction identifier and the trade identifier together. The last three belong in the key deliberately: two companies sharing two addresses are two pieces of evidence, not one duplicate, and collapsing them on entity pair and type alone would erase the second and understate the connection.

How this pattern looks

Engine sample · synthetic data

A worked example, step by step: one entity of NEXUS's own synthetic sample records, eight rows NEXUS's adapter hands fc-03's FND002 engine, and the links the engine discovered for it. Every company is fictional. The engine finds links; it gives no score and no band, so each step says only whether the engine's output holds a link of that kind for the subject. A link is not a finding of wrongdoing: as this page's detection logic says, two companies at one serviced address share an address and may share nothing else, and the engine does not decide whether a shared address or contact means common control. The subject is chosen by NEXUS's adapter, not by the engine: it is the entity with the most distinct linked entities, and where entities tie, the one whose identifier sorts last as text. Three companies tie here at two linked entities each. The companies' names come from those same NEXUS sample records through its case; the engine reads no name. This is the engine's own output for one sample entity, not a customer case.

Each step: what the engine states, and the rule behind it

  • found the engine's output holds a link of that kind for the subject
  • not found the engine looked and its output holds none for the subject
  • not stated the engine was given nothing to find it from

  1. Shared registered address

    found

    What the engine statesThe engine's output holds shared-address links from the subject to 2 other entities: to Northgate Trading Ltd (E01), Southgate Imports Ltd (E02) on the address value 50 broad street london, as the engine writes it. Each carries confidence 0.90 and evidence source Address Match. Across the whole run the output holds 10 shared-address rows, which are 5 links, each held once in each direction

    The ruleAddress pass: the engine reads each record's address, trims it and writes it in lower case, and links every two records that give the same text, with one row in each direction. A record with no address links to none, and an address that only one record gives makes no link. Addresses that differ by a character, by the spacing between words or by punctuation do not match. Every address link carries a fixed confidence of 0.90 and the evidence source Address Match: 0.90 is the engine's constant, the same for every address link, and not a measure of how likely this link is to matter. The engine does not know what an address is, so a serviced office shared by many companies links them all alike. The step is found where the engine returned such a link with the subject at one end, and not found where it returned none

  2. The linked entities link to each other

    found

    What the engine statesThe engine's output also links the entities linked to the subject to each other: Northgate Trading Ltd (E01) and Southgate Imports Ltd (E02) on the address value 50 broad street london, as the engine writes it. Each carries confidence 0.90 and evidence source Address Match

    The ruleAll-pairs rule: both passes link every two records that give the same value, so when the subject and other entities give one value, the engine also links those other entities to each other. The step is found where the output holds a link between two entities that are both linked to the subject, and not found where it holds none. It cannot be stated for a subject with fewer than two linked entities, which have none to link to each other

  3. Shared contact detail

    not found

    What the engine statesThe engine's output holds no shared-contact link for the subject. Across the whole run it holds 4 shared-contact rows, which are 2 links (the engine holds each link once in each direction), none of them the subject's

    The ruleContact pass: the engine reads four fields of a record, email, phone, contact number and domain, trims each and writes it in lower case, and links every two records that give the same text in any of them, with one row in each direction (a value one record gives as an email and another gives as a phone counts as the same). Every contact link carries a fixed confidence of 0.88 and the evidence source Contact Match. The records NEXUS gives the engine have email and phone columns only, so the engine read two of the four fields. The step is found where the engine returned such a link with the subject at one end, and not found where it returned none

  4. Links from directors and owners

    not stated

    What the engine statesThe engine's output holds no link from a director or an owner, for any entity. It could not hold one: the records NEXUS gives the engine are flat rows with no directors or owners list. So its silence on these two kinds says nothing about the subject

    The ruleDirector and ownership passes: the engine links a director to a company from a directors list inside a record, and an owner to a company from an owners list inside a record. It builds those only from the lists it is given. This step can be neither found nor cleared here, because the engine was given no such list

  5. Links from transactions and trades

    not stated

    What the engine statesThe engine's output holds no link from a transaction or a trade, for any entity. It could not hold one: NEXUS gives the engine no transaction records. So its silence on these two kinds says nothing about the subject

    The ruleTransaction and trade passes: the engine links two entities from each transaction record it is given, and links them again, as a trade, where the record's type is TRADE. It builds those only from the transaction records it is given, and NEXUS's adapter gives it none. This step can be neither found nor cleared here, because the engine was given no transaction record

Westgate Holdings Ltd is the subject NEXUS's adapter chose. The engine gives no score and no band, for the subject or for any entity. The run's confidence figure, 0.8943, is NEXUS's adapter's mean of the confidence of all 14 link rows, each of which the engine fixes at 0.90 (address) or 0.88 (contact); it is not a measure of the subject. The run's 14 relationships are 7 links, each held once in each direction, between 5 pairs of entities: 2 of those pairs share both an address and a contact. 2 of the 5 steps found something; 1 found nothing and 2 could not be stated from the engine's output.
