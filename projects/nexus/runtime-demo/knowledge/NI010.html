Hidden Controller

Who is actually controlling the entity or network?

Supporting questions:

Does a hidden controller exist?

Who benefits from the activity?

Who influences decisions?

Who controls multiple connected entities?

Does declared ownership differ from actual control?

Is the structure intentionally concealing control?

NI010 Hidden Controller identifies individuals or entities exercising effective control over an organisation despite not appearing as the declared owner, director or controller.

The purpose of this intelligence product is to detect:

  • Hidden beneficial ownership
  • Undisclosed control structures
  • Proxy ownership arrangements
  • Nominee director structures
  • Sanctions evasion schemes
  • Concealed influence networks
  • Financial crime control structures

NI010 represents the highest-value Network Intelligence capability because it seeks to identify who truly controls a network.
