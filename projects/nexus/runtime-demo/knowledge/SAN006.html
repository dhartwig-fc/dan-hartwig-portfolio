SAN006 · Sanctions

Trade Based Sanctions Evasion

Trade-Based Sanctions Evasion moves value or prohibited goods across a sanctioned boundary by manipulating the trade transaction itself — mispricing an invoice, or misdescribing the goods so that a controlled item travels under an innocuous label. The transaction is the instrument of evasion.

The finding is a trade-anomaly finding: an abnormal deviation between declared and benchmark price, and/or a mismatch between declared and actual goods, weighted against the destination, the counterparty and any ownership link to a designated party.

Risk indicators

Trade-Anomaly

  • Large price deviation from benchmark (over/under-pricing)
  • Goods misdescription (declared ≠ actual)
  • High-value cargo under generic descriptions

Jurisdiction

  • Sanctioned destination

Counterparty

  • Counterparty sanctions exposure or adverse media

Ownership

  • Trading party owned by / linked to a designated party

Investigation actions

  1. Resolve the trade, its parties and counterparties.
  2. Compare declared unit price against benchmark; grade the deviation.
  3. Compare declared against actual goods for misdescription.
  4. Score jurisdiction exposure for the destination.
  5. Score counterparty exposure and any ownership link to a designated party.
  6. Aggregate; escalate at or above the threshold.

Detection logic
