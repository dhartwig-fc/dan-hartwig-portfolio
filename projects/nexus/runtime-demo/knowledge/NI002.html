Shared Beneficial Owner

Do two or more entities share the same beneficial owner?

Supporting questions:

Who ultimately controls the entities?

Is ownership direct or indirect?

What percentage ownership exists?

Does ownership create sanctions exposure?

Does ownership indicate related-party activity?

Is ownership disclosed?

NI002 Shared Beneficial Owner identifies entities connected through a common beneficial owner, ultimate beneficial owner (UBO), controlling shareholder or ultimate controller.

The purpose of this intelligence product is to identify common ownership structures that may indicate:

  • Related-party relationships
  • Undisclosed ownership connections
  • Hidden control structures
  • Trade-based financial crime exposure
  • Sanctions exposure
  • Correspondent banking exposure
  • Market manipulation exposure

NI002 is one of the most important Network Intelligence products because beneficial ownership often represents the true control layer within a network.

Risk indicators

Risk

  • Shared UBO between trading entities
  • Undisclosed related-party relationship
  • Ownership through offshore structure
  • Ownership connected to sanctioned entity
  • Ownership linked to adverse media
  • Complex ownership layering
